## Serving conversions
For the published US-ounce recipes, the game uses rounded bar measures of 30 ml per ounce and states this in the card. This is a practical game serving, not a claim that the exact physical US fluid ounce is 30 ml.

The base IBA seeds are unchanged. Source ranges such as a few dashes are represented explicitly; garnish optionality is preserved. House additions and serving choices are declared. Original screenshot wording remains in requirements.md.

No ingredient source, supplier note or delivery record is shown to the player. Recipe citations explain preparation references; they are distinct from the ingredient-origin social-bluff mechanic.
